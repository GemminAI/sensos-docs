## Abstract

Cross-cutting framework linking RFC requirements to implementations, tests, evidence, and certification claims. Applies to **all** SensOS RFC namespaces and products.

## Normative reference

Canonical draft: `RFCv3_draft/rfc/ITM/RFC-ITM001.md`  
Series index: `RFCv3_draft/rfc/ITM/RFC_ITM_SERIES_INDEX.md`  
Namespace map: [RFC Namespaces](/Open-Standards/Namespaces)

## Independence

RFC-ITM is product-independent. It applies equally to CORE, HEXT, HEKB, MCP, CAL, SensOS, Meaning Mapper (RFC-MM), and future products.
