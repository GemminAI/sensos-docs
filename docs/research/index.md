# Research

Research tracks that inform SensOS specifications. Research documents may be Draft or Experimental; they are not automatically normative.

## How to use this section

1. Treat research notes as **inputs** to the RFC process.
2. Confirm promotion status in [RFC_MAP](../rfc/RFC_MAP.md) before citing as Depends On.
3. Prefer [Papers](../papers/index.md) for published / archival write-ups.

## Active themes

| Theme | Summary | Related RFCs |
|---|---|---|
| Projection verification | Statistical hypotheses for observation→manifold mapping | [RFC-0100](../rfc/RFC-0100.md) |
| Observation / Projection ABIs | Boundary separation (AMS-0001 lineage) | [RFC-0200](../rfc/RFC-0200.md), [RFC-0201](../rfc/RFC-0201.md) |
| HEKB research | Manifold, retrieval, MCP proposals (non-product-core until promoted) | [RFC-0203](../rfc/RFC-0203.md), [HEKB](../hekb/index.md) |
| Conformance evidence | Experimental support for CORE/ENHANCED gates | [RFC-0001](../rfc/RFC-0001.md) |

## Status discipline

| If the artifact is… | Cite as |
|---|---|
| Draft research note | Informative only |
| Accepted RFC | Provisional / review-bound (see RFC_MAP) |
| Implemented / CI Verified / Released | Implementation-mapping eligible |

## Contribution

Open research PRs against [sensos-docs](https://github.com/GemminAI/sensos-docs) under `docs/research/` or propose promotion into `docs/rfc/`.
